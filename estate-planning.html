Estate Planning

Thoughtful, effective estate plans designed to protect your assets and provide peace of mind for the future.

At Prudential Advocate, we help individuals and families create thoughtful, effective estate plans that protect their assets and provide peace of mind for the future.

Estate planning is more than drafting documents — it is about making informed decisions that reflect your values, your priorities, and the legacy you want to leave. We take a personalized approach, working closely with you to understand your circumstances and craft a plan that is both comprehensive and practical.

How we help

We prepare a full range of estate-planning documents, including wills, revocable living trusts, powers of attorney, and advance health care directives.

Each plan is carefully tailored so your assets are distributed according to your wishes, trusted people are in place to make decisions when needed, and potential complications are minimized. Addressing these issues proactively helps you avoid unnecessary court involvement and reduces the likelihood of future disputes.

Our approach

We also advise on more advanced strategies, including asset protection, tax considerations, and planning for minor children or beneficiaries with special needs.

We take the time to explain each option in clear, understandable terms so you can make confident decisions. As laws and personal circumstances change, we are available to review and update your plan so it stays effective over time.

What to expect

Our goal is to make estate planning straightforward and accessible while delivering lasting value.

Clients rely on us for clear communication, attention to detail, and a commitment to getting it right. With a well-structured plan in place, you can move forward knowing your loved ones are protected and your wishes will be honored.

These matters can feel overwhelming — we explain your options in plain terms and provide candid advice about risks and likely outcomes. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes.

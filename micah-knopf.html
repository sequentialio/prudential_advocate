About Micah Knopf

Micah Knopf is a U.S. Army veteran and USC graduate whose professional interests span law, business, commercial real estate, and public service.

Micah began his career in the United States Army, enlisting at 17 as a Cannon Crewmember. He advanced quickly through the ranks and was promoted to Sergeant at 19, taking on responsibility for leading soldiers, coordinating operations, and training junior service members. His military experience strengthened his ability to lead under pressure, adapt to changing circumstances, and remain accountable for both people and outcomes.

He later served in the California National Guard as a Paralegal Specialist, supporting military legal matters involving administrative actions, disciplinary proceedings, legal research, and case-file preparation. In that role, Micah assisted service members, reviewed legal documents, and gained practical exposure to military justice and federal law.

Following his military service, Micah earned a degree in Legal Studies from the University of Southern California. He has also gained professional experience in human resources, legal support, project coordination, and organizational operations. These roles expanded his understanding of employment matters, business law, workplace policy, and the relationship between legal and business decision-making.

Micah is continuing his graduate education at USC while pursuing opportunities in law, commercial real estate, investment, and business development. He is particularly interested in acquisitions, development, government contracting, and projects that create long-term economic and community impact.

In addition to his professional pursuits, Micah is the founder of M.A.K Consult Holdings LLC, a California-based company created to explore government contracting, consulting, business acquisitions, and other entrepreneurial opportunities. His long-term goal is to build and lead organizations that combine disciplined execution, responsible growth, and meaningful service to others.

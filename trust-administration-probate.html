Trust Administration & Probate

Practical, step-by-step guidance for trustees, executors, administrators, and beneficiaries — in and out of court.

At Prudential Advocate, we guide trustees, executors, administrators, and beneficiaries through every stage of administering a loved one’s trust or estate — with clarity, efficiency, and careful attention to detail.

Some estates are administered privately through a trust; others pass through probate, the court-supervised process of validating a will, gathering assets, paying debts, and distributing property. Many families face both at once. We handle each path — and help you understand which one applies to your situation.

Trust administration

Administering a trust in California involves a range of legal and financial responsibilities — from identifying and managing assets to communicating with beneficiaries and ensuring compliance with the law. We assist trustees with every part of the process, including interpreting trust terms, preparing required notices, handling asset transfers, and coordinating distributions.

Our goal is to see the trust administered properly and in keeping with the settlor’s intent. With proactive guidance, we help trustees avoid common pitfalls that can lead to disputes, delays, or personal liability. We also advise beneficiaries who have questions about how a trust is being managed — helping you understand your rights, review accountings, and, when necessary, take action to ensure transparency and compliance.

Probate

When an estate must go through probate, we assist personal representatives with every aspect of the administration, from filing the initial petition to final distribution and closing the estate. That includes preparing court filings, coordinating appraisals, addressing creditor claims, and ensuring proper notice to all interested parties.

For beneficiaries and heirs, we provide guidance and advocacy to protect your rights throughout the process — whether there are questions about a will’s validity, concerns about how the estate is being handled, or disputes among interested parties.

What to expect

Trust and estate administration usually follows the loss of a loved one, making it both a legal and a personal experience. We emphasize communication, organization, and efficiency — streamlining each step and providing steady guidance from start to finish, so you can move forward knowing your responsibilities are handled correctly and your interests are well protected.

These matters can feel overwhelming — we explain your options in plain terms and provide candid advice about risks and likely outcomes. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes.

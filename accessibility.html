Our Commitment

Prudential Advocate is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

Measures We Take

We design and test for keyboard accessibility, sufficient color contrast, descriptive text alternatives for images, clear headings and labels, and compatibility with assistive technologies such as screen readers.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we regularly review the site to identify and resolve barriers.

Reporting a Barrier

If you encounter any difficulty using this website, or need information in an alternative format, please contact us — we want to help and to fix the issue. Please describe the problem and the page so we can respond promptly.
